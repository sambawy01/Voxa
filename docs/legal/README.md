# Legal pages

The terms, privacy policy and refund policy are public pages, so Paddle's seller review and
your customers can read them:

- `web/terms.html` at `/terms`
- `web/privacy.html` at `/privacy`
- `web/refunds.html` at `/refunds`
- `web/pricing.html` at `/pricing`

The operator's name and contact email come from the server's settings, `VOXA_OPERATOR` and
`VOXA_CONTACT_EMAIL`, and the price from `VOXA_PRICE_LABEL`. Until they are set, the pages
show placeholders in brackets.

These texts were written from what the code does. They are not legal advice. Have them
reviewed for the countries Voxa serves before launch: EU and UK consumer law and GDPR, and
the transfer of podcast text to DeepSeek in China. Update them whenever the data Voxa handles
changes.
