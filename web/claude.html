Voxa with Claude

Ask Claude for a podcast or an audiobook. Claude writes the script; Voxa's voices read it, right on your own device. Free, with no key and no limit: nothing is made on our servers.

  • In the chatClaude shows the Voxa player in the conversation: press Play, then save the MP3. Where a Claude app cannot show the player, Claude gives you a link that opens it here.
  • On your computerClaude Code or Claude Desktop runs Voxa on your computer and saves finished MP3s in your Music folder.

In the chat Free

  1. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Voxa and paste this address: 
    https://voxa-zpukmw.fly.dev/mcp
  3. In a new chat, turn on Voxa and ask, for example: “Use Voxa to make a two-minute podcast about coffee, and play it.”

The player appears in the chat with a card for each speaker. The first time, your browser downloads the voices (tens of MB); after that they are kept. Your words and the audio stay in your browser. If the chat app blocks the download, press Open in Voxa and save the MP3 there.

On your computer Free

  1. Install uv, a small tool that runs Voxa with everything it needs: 
    curl -LsSf https://astral.sh/uv/install.sh | sh
    powershell -ExecutionPolicy ByPass -c "irm https://astral.sh/uv/install.ps1 | iex"
  2. Connect Claude.

    Claude Code: run

    claude mcp add voxa -- uv run https://voxa-zpukmw.fly.dev/voxa-mcp.py

    Claude Desktop: Settings → Developer → Edit Config, and put this in the file:

    {"mcpServers": {"voxa": {"command": "uv", "args": ["run", "https://voxa-zpukmw.fly.dev/voxa-mcp.py"]}}}

    On a Mac, if Claude Desktop says it cannot find uv, use its full path: run which uv in Terminal (it is usually /Users/you/.local/bin/uv) and put that in place of "uv". Then restart Claude Desktop.

  3. Ask Claude: “Use Voxa to read this article aloud in Lina’s voice.” The MP3 lands in Music → Voxa.

The first time, Voxa downloads its voices (111 MB) from this site; after that it works offline. Voices on your computer: ten voices in 31 languages, including Arabic. To check it works, run uv run https://voxa-zpukmw.fly.dev/voxa-mcp.py --check: it says one sentence into Music → Voxa.

Things to ask

  • Make a podcast with three hosts about this PDF, in an expressive style.
  • Turn my notes into a five-minute episode and play it here.
  • Read chapter one of this story aloud, with a calm British voice.
  • Make the same episode in Arabic, with a man and a woman hosting.
  • Change line 4 so Leo sounds more surprised, and make it again.

Claude gets these Voxa tools: play_on_device (the player in the chat), list_device_voices, and on your computer create_podcast, create_audiobook, make_speech, edit_line, get_job and open_audio. Voices are synthetic: when you share what you make, say it is AI.
