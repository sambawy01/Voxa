Good to know

  • Switching between models never downloads a model again or makes a second copy: each one is saved once.
  • When Voxa updates a model, the old version is deleted as the new one is saved.
  • A deleted model downloads again the next time you use one of its voices.
  • Browsers can clear saved files when the device is low on space, or after a site isn't used for a while (Safari). Keep voices on this device asks your browser not to, where it supports that.
  • Your saved audiobooks and podcasts are kept separately, on the Audiobooks and Podcasts pages, and are not touched here.
