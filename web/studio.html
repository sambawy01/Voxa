Replace words inside a recording

Give it a recording and the sentence you want instead. Only the words that change are re-spoken, in the same voice; the rest of the recording is kept exactly. The recording stays on this device.

Projects

A project is the cast and the scene. Saved on this device; export a file to keep or move it.
